Case Study

Boeing + Everett Community College + CityU

Bachelor of Arts in Management (BAM)

Challenge

Boeing employees needed a flexible pathway to complete bachelor's degrees while balancing work and family responsibilities.

Solution

A partnership between Boeing, Everett Community College, and City University of Seattle created a workforce-focused degree pathway.

Impact

2,000+ participants have benefited from the BAM model.

How It Works

A three-way partnership model

BAM works because each partner contributes what it does best: the employer provides workplace context, the community college provides technical certificates, and CityU provides the degree pathway.

Employer Role

Boeing identifies eligible employees and supports participation through tuition benefits.

Community College Role

Everett Community College delivers professional certificate coursework that feeds into the degree.

University Role

CityU evaluates certificate and prior credit, then delivers the remaining bachelor's coursework.

Considering a similar model for your organization?

BAM shows what's possible with a three-way employer, community college, and university partnership. Let's talk about what that could look like for you.
