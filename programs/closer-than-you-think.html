What's Included

Every path back to a degree starts with an honest credit count

Transfer Credit Evaluation

A full review of prior college coursework, wherever it was earned.

Prior Learning Recognition

Credit consideration for verified workplace and professional learning.

Military Credit Review

Evaluation of military training and service against degree requirements.

Flexible Learning

Coursework structured around full-time work and family schedules.

Accelerated Degree Completion

A plan built to close the remaining credit gap as directly as possible.

Advising Support

A dedicated point of contact through enrollment and completion.

Who It's For

Built for employers with stalled-out degree-seekers on staff

If employees started a degree and stopped, this program exists to find out how close they actually are — and build a plan to close the gap.

1

Connect

Employees connect with a CityU advisor to start the evaluation.

2

Evaluate

Transfer, prior learning, and military credit are reviewed together.

3

Plan

A completion plan is built around the credit that actually applies.

4

Enroll

Employees begin coursework on a flexible, work-compatible schedule.

5

Complete

Employees finish the degree they started.
