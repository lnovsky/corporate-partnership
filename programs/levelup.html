Leadership Growth

LevelUP™

Helping educational achievement catch up with career growth. LevelUP™ is built for employees who are already leading — informally or formally — and need the credential and skill development to match.

What's Included

Built for managers and future leaders

Leadership Development

Coursework and coaching built around real management scenarios, not abstract theory.

Manager Growth

Structured development for first-time and mid-level managers stepping into bigger roles.

Succession Planning

Give your organization a documented bench of ready-now leadership candidates.

Degree Completion

Leadership coursework that counts toward a completed degree, not a standalone certificate.

Graduate Pathways

A direct route into graduate-level study for employees ready to go further.

Flexible Scheduling

Coursework designed to fit around full-time work, not compete with it.

Who It's For

Built for employers investing in their next layer of leadership

LevelUP™ fits organizations that have identified high-potential employees but lack a structured, credentialed path to develop them.

1

Identify

Your organization identifies high-potential employees for the pathway.

2

Assess

CityU evaluates prior credit and maps a plan to their target credential.

3

Enroll

Employees begin coursework built around live management challenges.

4

Apply

Coursework is applied directly to real leadership responsibilities.

5

Advance

Employees graduate into expanded leadership roles inside your organization.
